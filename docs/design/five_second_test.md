# 5-second test script

Run with 3-5 people who have not seen the site. Use the live site on a phone (375px) for half of them and a laptop for the other half. Record results in the table. Do not explain the site beforehand.

## Setup
1. Open `/` (Public), then `/?view=analyst` for a second round with different people. One person sees one page only.
2. Show the page for exactly 5 seconds, then hide it (lock the screen or close the tab).
3. Ask the three questions in order, one at a time. Write down their words, not your paraphrase. Do not prompt or correct.

## Questions
1. "What is this page about?"
2. "Which hospital needs attention?"
3. "How old is the data?"

## Scoring
- Q1 passes if they say hospital emergency departments, bed waits or waiting in the ED. A clinic finder alone is a miss for the Analyst view. For Public, record it as "clinic" or "ED" and do not score it (decision pending, review N5).
- Q2 passes if they name the hospital the page marks as higher (not the longest wait unless it is also marked). They may say "I didn't see".
- Q3 passes if they say about when the data is from within a week. "I couldn't tell" counts as a fail.
