## 4. Keyboard and screen-reader walk

Automated walk (Playwright Tab x up to 120, six view and width combinations) plus axe from the e2e suite:
- Skip link is the first stop on every page. Header, view switch, hospital links and controls follow in reading order.
- Every focus stop has a visible ring (0 stops without an outline or shadow). No zero-size focus stops.
- Every control has an accessible name. The only controls without text were radios and the postcode input, all named by their `<label>`.
- At 375px the Menu button is the third stop, so the nav sheet is reachable.

Not done, and not claimed: I did not run VoiceOver or NVDA. Items for you to listen for (TODO(me)):
